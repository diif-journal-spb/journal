Partial integrals of real polynomial
partial di erential systems of the  rst order
A.F. Pranevich, O.N. Belko, Y u.Y u. Gnezdovsky , P .B. Pavlyuchik
Y ank a Kupala State University of Grodno
{pranevich, belko_on, gnezdovsky , p.pavlyuchik}@grsu.by
Abstract In this paper we consider real polynomial systems of partial di erential equations
of the  rst order. The method of building real  rst integrals and last multipliers by known
real and complex-v alued polynomial partial integrals, taking into account their multiplicity ,
and conditional partial integrals has been developed. The in uence of Jacobian conditions
on the possibility of constructing  rst integrals and last multipliers by partial integrals of
partial di erential system is shown. F unctional relationships between the coordinate functions
of cofactors of partial integrals of complete and Jacobian systems of partial di erential equations
are established. The problems of modifying  rst integrals and last multipliers of the Darboux
type are solved. Su cient conditions of constructing  rst integrals for partial di erential systems
by two known partial integrals are obtained. The possibility of constructing  rst integrals
for normal partial di erential systems by a known partial integral is considered. Examples
of systems of partial di erential equations of the  rst order are given, which illustrate the
theoretical studies carried out in this article. The results can be applied in the analytical
theory of di erential equations and applied problems of mechanics, optics and hydrodynamics.
Key words : system of partial di erential equations, complete partial di erential system,
Jacobian partial di erential system,  rst integral, real partial integral, complex-v alued partial
integral, multiplicity , conditional partial integral, last multiplier, last pseudo-multiplier.
