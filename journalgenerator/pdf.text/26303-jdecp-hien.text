Abstract. In this paper, the problems of stability and stabilization via static
output-feedback control are extended for positive continuous-time Roesser
modeldescribedCaputoderivativeswithincommensurateorders. Ageneralso-
lution representation to state-space equation of the model is first given. Then,
using the established solution representation, necessary and sufficient condi-
tions ensuring the positivity of the system (i.e. all system states initiated from
nonnegative states are always nonnegative for all times) are derived. By uti-
lizing the monotonicity induced from the positivity, a necessary and sufficient
stability condition is formulated in terms of a tractable LP condition. The ob-
tained stability result is then taken into deriving LP-based conditions for the
existence of a desired controller that makes the closed-loop system positive and
stable.

1 Introduction
Two-dimensional(2-D)systemsarewidelyusedtodescribedynamicsofvarious
practical and physical processes where the information propagation occurs in
each of the two independent directions [1]. For example, a number of partial
differential equations (PDEs) with two independent variables appearing in gas
absorption, heat exchangers or image processing can be converted into 2-D sys-
tems which are important for realizing control processes [2–4]. Among various
models of 2-D systems, Roesser model [5] has found practical applications in
numerous areas such as 2-D control systems [6], digital filter structures [7], lin-
ear repetitive processes [8,9], or iterative learning control [10–12]. Due to a
wide range of applications, the theory of 2-D systems has received significant
research attention in the past few years. In particular, various problems in the
systems and control theory including stability, performance analysis and con-
troller/filter synthesis have been extensively studied for 2-D systems described
by Roesser-type models. To mention a few, we refer the reader to existing
works [13–19].
Recently, theproblemsofstabilityanalysisandcontrolof2-Dsystemswith
or without delays have received remarkable attention and a number of results
have been reported in the literature. While the theory of 2-D discrete-time
systems has been well studied and developed, there have been only a few
results devoted to the study of 2-D systems in the continuous-time domain.
For example, the problems of H
∞
stabilization and reliable H
∞
stabilization
via state-feedback controllers were studied for some classes of 2-D continuous-
time Roesser systems with time-varying delays in [20,21] and [22], respectively.
Based on a variant of the Lyapunov–Krasovskii functional (LKF) method, suf-
ficient conditions ensuring asymptotic stability with H
∞
performance of the
closed-loop systems were derived in a linear matrix inequality (LMI) setting.
The problem of delay-dependent exponential stability of 2-D continuous-time
Roessersystemswasalsoinvestigatedin[23]. Morerecently, theproblemofex-
ponential stabilization of 2-D continuous-time Roesser systems with directional
input delays was considered in [24] for the first time. Based on a modified re-
duction method and by utilizing the extended LKF method proposed in [23],
analysisandsynthesisconditionsofaprediction-basedstate-feedbackcontroller
were derived in the form of tractable LMIs, which can be effectively solved by
various computational tools to obtain desired controller gains.
On the other hand, fractional calculus with a long history as a pure math-
Electronic Journal: http://diffjournal.spbu.ru/ 85Differential Equations and Control Processes, N. 3, 2026
ematical field is most suitable to use in description of hereditary properties of
various materials and processes which are neglected in classical integer models.
In particular, fractional-order (FrO) differential equations have found applica-
tions in many areas such as viscoelastic, dielectric polarization, electromag-
netic waves, heat conduction, robotics or circuit systems [25–27]. In the past
two decades, the study of FrO dynamical 1-D systems has attracted consid-
erable attention with various interesting results have been reported [28–30].
Such studies for FrO 2-D systems have received considerably less research at-
tention, especially for 2-D continuous-time systems. The notion of FrO 2-D
discrete systems was introduced and studied in [31]. Continuous 2-D FrO sys-
tems in the Roesser structure and their extensions were introduced in [26,32]
and state-space equations involving partial FrO Caputo derivative were pre-
sented. Recently, the problem of positivity analysis was developed for contin-
uous 2-D Fornasini-Marchesini fractional model in [33]. A representation of
general solutions is obtained by using certain type of Laplace transformations.
By employing the obtained solution formula, positivity of the system is dis-
cussed. However, due to substantial differences in their structures, the results
of [33] are generally not applicable for FrO 2-D continuous Roesser systems.
Extending such studies for 2-D Roesser systems proves to be more challenging
and evolving, which motivates further investigation.
Inthispaper, weconsideraclassof2-Dsystemsdescribedbyacontinuous-
time Roesser model via Caputo derivatives with incommensurate orders. The
addressed problem consists of the following aspects. First, we employ a 2-
D Laplace transformation to formulate a general solution representation. We
then derive conditions that characterize the positivity of the system. Third,
by utilizing the monotonicity induced from the positivity, we exploit necessary
and sufficient conditions in terms of tractable linear programing (LP) settings
to ensure the system stability. Finally, the obtained stability result is utilized
to address the design problem of static output-feedback controllers (SOFCs)
that make the corresponding closed-loop system positive and stable.

8 Conclusion
The problems of positivity, stability analysis and static output-feedback con-
troller design have been extended for continuous-time Roesser model described
Caputo fractional-order derivatives. A general solution representation to state-
space equation of the model has been obtained using 2-D Laplace transforma-
tions and the positivity of the system has been discussed. It is shown that a
fractional-order continuous-time 2-D Roesser system is positive if and only if
the state matrix is Metzler and other system matrices are nonnegative. The
positivity has been then utilized to derive LP-stability and stabilization condi-
tions. Numerical simulations have been provided to validate the effectiveness
of the proposed method in this paper.
