High-precision modeling of an electric  eld in an electroplating bath
by the method of lines and power series
A. N. Pchelintsev
T ambov State T echnical University
e-mail: pchelintsev.an@yandex.ru
Abstract. The paper considers numerical modeling of a stationary electric
 eld in a rectangular electroplating bath. The original boundary-v alue problem
for the Laplace equation includes insulating boundary conditions on the bath
walls, a prescribed potential on the anode, and a nonlinear cathode condition
relating the normal deriv ative of the potential to the cathodic polarization curve.
The method of lines is used:  nite-di erence approximation is introduced only
along the coordinate directed along the electrodes, and the two-dimensional
problem is reduced to a boundary-v alue problem for a system of ordinary
di erential equations. The system is solved by a numerical-analytical power-series
method, while unknown initial data are found by a shooting procedure based
on minimization of the boundary residual function. Special attention is paid to
the numerical experiment for a bath with a  at anode and cathode. The paper
presents computational parameters, a stopping criterion for the power series, the
calculated distribution of nickel coating thickness, and an estimate explaining
why arbitrary-precision arithmetic is required when the size of the computational
domain increases.
Keywords: Laplace equation, electroplating bath, method of lines, power series,
shooting method, arbitrary-precision arithmetic, numerical experiment.
