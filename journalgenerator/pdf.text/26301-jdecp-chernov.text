On solv ability of a game problem consisting in neutralization of
perturbation of an evolution equation with a nonstationary operator
Chernov A.V.
∗1
∗ Nizhnii Novgorod State University named after N.I.Lobachevskii
1
e-mail: chavnn@mail.ru
Abstract. W e consider a game problem consisting in neutralization of perturba-
tion of a semilinear evolutionary equation with nonstationary linear operator in a
Hilbert space. W e suppose that the right-hand side of equation depends linearly
on two controls and one of them is treated as some perturbation while anoth-
er is treated as the neutralizing action of the operating player. It is required to
carry over the controlled system to a given state at a given time moment in the
presence of perturbation. Both controls are supposed bounded by the norm. As
is well known, in such a form one can present also pursuit games. W e establish
su cient conditions for solv ability of the determined game problem in both cases
of linear and nonlinear equation. Here we use the Minty-Browder’s theorem and
also a chain technology of successive continuation of the solution to a controlled
system to intermediate states. As example of reduction to the abstract operator
equation under study we consider the semilinear equation of global electric circuit
in the Earth atmosphere and a semilinear pseudoparabolic equation.
Key words: semilinear evolutionary equation in a Hilbert space, nonstationary
operator, conditions for solv ability of a game problem, neutralization of pertur-
bation.
